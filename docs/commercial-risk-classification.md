# Commercial-risk classification

Commercial risk describes consequences if a website is misunderstood or trusted without enough evidence. It is not a technical SEO penalty.

| Classification | Typical context | Evidence expectation | Report treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LOW_CONSEQUENCE | informational, low-commitment | normal identity and content evidence | standard priority |
| MODERATE_CONSEQUENCE | local services, B2B purchasing | stronger contact, service, and policy evidence | elevated trust recommendations |
| HIGH_CONSEQUENCE | property, construction, substantial payments | project, ownership, credential, dispute, and payment evidence | urgent trust gaps |
| REGULATED_OR_SENSITIVE | healthcare, finance, legal, safety | authoritative credentials, legal disclosures, provenance | highest evidence threshold |

Classification affects recommendation urgency, verification requirements, and trust-warning language. It must not directly reduce unrelated metrics such as canonical validity, page response, or image optimization.
