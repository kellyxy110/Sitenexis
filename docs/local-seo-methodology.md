# Local SEO methodology

## Activation

Local analysis activates when location, service-area, local-business schema, local intent, or connected business-profile evidence is present. Otherwise it is NOT_APPLICABLE or PARTIAL, not a failure.

## Evidence matrix

| Signal | Evidence layers | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAP | HTML, schema, external profile | compare values and show conflicts |
| Address | HTML, image-only, schema, external | label the source; image-only is not confirmed text |
| Google Business Profile/maps | connected/external provider | absence requires external-data qualification |
| Service area/location pages | content, schema, sitemap | distinguish pages from unsupported claims |
| Reviews/local citations | review/directory sources | record source and independence |
| Geographic content | title, headings, body, schema | assess relevance, not keyword volume |

Unavailable external sources produce EXTERNAL_DATA_REQUIRED or PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE. Do not say a business has no local presence without sufficient external checks.
