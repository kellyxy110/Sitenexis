# Benchmark classification

Benchmarking begins only after business-category classification.

## Supported categories

Real estate, construction, local services, SaaS, education, healthcare, finance, marketplace, publishing, professional services, and e-commerce are the initial taxonomy.

## Classification contract

The classifier records primary category, secondary categories, evidence, and confidence. It uses title, headings, services, schema, dominant entities, content, location, and verified external profiles. A weak or conflicting classification blocks category-specific benchmarking and reports PARTIAL or CONFLICTING instead.

Benchmark profiles specify expected evidence, not an assumed score. For example, construction may prioritize projects, credentials, safety, service areas, and enquiry paths. SaaS may prioritize product documentation, pricing clarity, integration evidence, and trust pages. Categories must never be benchmarked against unrelated categories.
